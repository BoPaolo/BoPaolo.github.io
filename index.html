A Numerical Investigation of Turbulent Flow Dynamics in Complex Geometries

Nome Cognome, A. Researcher, B. Scientist

Submitted to Journal of Fluid Mechanics

[Publisher] ↓ [PDF]
Abstract. We investigate the dynamics of turbulent flows developing in complex geometries using high-fidelity numerical simulations. The analysis focuses on the interaction between large-scale coherent structures and small-scale turbulent fluctuations. The results provide new insights into the mechanisms governing momentum transport and flow separation.

Journal publications

Experimental and Numerical Analysis of Vortex Formation in Confined Flows

Nome Cognome, C. Author, D. Researcher

Journal of Computational Physics, Vol. 512, 2025

[Publisher] ↓ [PDF]
Abstract. This paper presents a combined experimental and numerical investigation of vortex formation in confined flows. Particular attention is given to the dependence of vortex dynamics on Reynolds number and geometric confinement. Numerical predictions are compared against experimental measurements and show excellent agreement.

Reduced-Order Modelling of Unsteady Aerodynamic Flows

Nome Cognome, E. Author

Physics of Fluids, Vol. 37, 2025

[Publisher] ↓ [PDF]
Abstract. A reduced-order modelling framework is introduced for the efficient prediction of unsteady aerodynamic flows. The proposed approach combines data-driven techniques with classical physical modelling and is demonstrated on several benchmark problems.
